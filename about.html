Our Legacy

Pioneers of the Frozen Frontier

Founded in 1998, Arctic Breeze was born from a passion for the bond between human and husky. Today, we are the leading center for Arctic exploration and sled dog racing.

From a Small Cabin to a World-Class Kennel

Our journey started with just six dogs and a dream. Over three decades, we've expanded our kennel to house over 120 elite huskies, each bred for endurance, intelligence, and a love for the trail.

Our Mission

To preserve the heritage of mushing while providing elite experiences.

Our Vision

Setting the global gold standard for sled dog welfare and Arctic tourism.

The Arctic Community

We don't just operate in the Arctic; we are part of its fabric. From collaborating with local indigenous communities to hosting winter festivals, Arctic Breeze is a hub for northern culture and heritage.

Safety & Gear

Thermal Armor

We provide Helly Hansen professional grade thermal suits capable of handling -40°C, ensuring your focus remains on the adventure, not the cold.

Satellite Comm

Every sled is equipped with Garmin InReach satellite communication for constant safety monitoring, even in the deepest wilderness.

Pre-Ride Briefing

A mandatory 30-minute safety and sled-driving workshop ensures you are ready for the trail and understand the language of the pack.

Stewards of the Ice

Our commitment to the environment goes beyond carbon offsets. We actively participate in glacier monitoring and wildlife protection projects, using our expeditions as platforms for Arctic research.
