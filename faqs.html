Your Guide

Everything you need to know before you fly

What should I wear for the tour?

While we provide high-grade thermal overalls, boots, and gloves, we recommend wearing wool base layers and a warm fleece or down jacket underneath. Avoid cotton as it retains moisture and can make you feel colder.

Is previous experience required?

No experience is necessary for most of our tours. Our professional mushers provide full safety briefings and training before every ride. For the "Full Day Expedition," a basic level of physical fitness is recommended.

Are children allowed on the sleds?

Yes! Children aged 4 and up are welcome on our "Forest Glider" and "Family Experience" tours. They will typically sit in the sled with an adult while a musher or experienced adult drives.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Safety is our top priority. If conditions (like severe blizzards or lack of snow) make mushing unsafe, we will notify you as soon as possible to reschedule your tour or provide a full refund.

Can I drive my own team?

Yes! Most of our adult tours are "self-drive," meaning you will be in control of your own sled after the briefing. A musher will lead the way on their own sled, and you will follow in their tracks.
